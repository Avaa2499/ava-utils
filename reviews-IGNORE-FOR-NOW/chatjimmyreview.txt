Strengths


    Module-oriented architecture: Your design is based on a clean modular architecture, where each module is responsible for a single task, and they communicate with each other through messages.

    Fault containment: You've planned for fault containment, which means that a failed operation in one module won't take down the entire system.

    Standardized communication: Your use of standardized message formats (e.g., MessagePack) and topics/calls ensures that modules can easily communicate with each other.

    Isolation: You've implemented process isolation, which means that each module runs in its own process, reducing the risk of crashes affecting other modules.

    Systemd integration: Your use of systemd for managing module lifecycle and socket activation is a great choice.

    Testing and quality assurance: Your plan includes automatic testing and quality assurance checks, which ensures that modules are thoroughly tested and reviewed before being released.
